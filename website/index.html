FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

How does pricing work?

BYT includes monthly coaching credits so you can chat with your AI coach regularly. Standard and Plus plans are available in-app — pick what fits how often you want to connect.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

Generic AI forgets you between sessions. BYT remembers your goals, check-in history, and coach profile — so every conversation builds on the last. It's coaching software, not a chat window.

Is my data private?

Yes. We don't sell your personal data. Your journal, check-ins, and coaching history are used only to power your experience. You can delete your account and data anytime.

Do I need to check in every single day?

Daily check-ins work best, but life happens. Even a few times a week builds useful patterns — and your coach adapts to whatever rhythm you keep.

Can I use voice instead of typing?

Absolutely. BYT supports voice coaching for natural, spoken conversations — great for when you're on the move or just don't feel like typing.

Is BYT available on iPhone?
