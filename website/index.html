FAQ

Questions, answered.

How is BYT different from ChatGPT?

A general chatbot answers questions and hands out advice. BYT coaches: it listens, asks the questions that help you think, and leaves the decisions with you. It also remembers your goals, values and commitments, so every conversation builds on the last.

How much does it cost?

BYT is free to download. You get a coaching allowance that refreshes every day, so you can talk with your coach regularly without counting messages.

Will it just tell me what to do?

No, unless you ask. If you want a perspective, it offers one or two as options to take or leave, then hands the decision back to you. Plans stick better when they're yours.

Is my data private?

Yes. There are no ads or tracking, and we never sell your data. Your conversations, check-ins and journal are used only to run the app for you. You can delete your account and all its data at any time in Settings.

Can I talk instead of typing?

Yes. Voice coaching lets you have a spoken conversation with the same coach, with the same memory as your text chats.

Is BYT a replacement for therapy?

No. BYT is a coach, not a therapist or a medical service, and it won't diagnose or treat anything. If you're struggling or feel unsafe, please contact a professional or your local emergency services. The coach will point you to support if a conversation suggests you might need it.
