BYT Privacy Policy
Your data

Delete your account

You can delete your BYT account and everything linked to it at any time. It's permanent, and it takes less than a minute.

Quickest

In the app

  1. Open BYT and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account & data.
  3. Confirm. Your account and all its data are deleted straight away, and you're signed out.
No longer have the app?

Ask us by email

  1. Email us from the address you use to sign in to BYT, so we can confirm it's your account.
  2. We'll delete your account and all its data, and reply to confirm, within 7 days.
Email a deletion request

Or write to eshaantr.mukku@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my BYT account and data”.

What gets deleted

Everything stored for your account, permanently:

  • Your account (name, email and sign-in)
  • Your profile and everything you shared when setting up
  • Goals, daily check-ins and journal entries
  • All coach conversations, text and voice
  • The coach's notes about you, including commitments
  • Your daily allowance record
Nothing is kept afterwards, apart from technical logs that hold no content from your account and expire automatically within 30 days. Reminders already scheduled on your phone stop when you sign out or uninstall the app. Deleting your account can't be undone; to use BYT again, you'd start a new account.

Want to delete only some things? You can delete individual goals and coach conversations in the app. See the Privacy Policy for more about how your data is handled.
