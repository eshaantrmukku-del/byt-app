1. Who we are

BYT (Build Your Tomorrow) is an AI coaching app made by Eshaan Mukku, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom. We are responsible for your personal data under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). You can reach us at eshaantr.mukku@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Only what the app needs to work for you:

  • Account: your name and email address. Your password is handled by Google's sign-in service; we never see it.
  • Your profile: what you choose to tell us when you set up, such as your date of birth, work, goals, lifestyle and what you're finding hard.
  • What you create: goals, daily check-ins (mood, happiness, stress, sleep, reflections and wins) and journal entries.
  • Coach conversations: your messages and the coach's replies. Voice conversations are saved the same way, as text.
  • Coaching notes: short notes the coach keeps so it can remember you between conversations, such as a summary of past sessions, how you like to be coached, what matters to you, and commitments you've made.
  • Your daily allowance: how much coaching you've used today, so we can apply the daily limit.
  • Technical logs: basic records of errors and response times, linked to an account ID rather than your name, so we can fix problems.

Voice audio is never stored. When you use voice coaching, your audio is sent to be transcribed and answered, then discarded. Only the text of the conversation is saved.

Reminders stay on your phone. Check-in reminders and coach nudges are scheduled by the app on your device. Your settings, such as reminder times, are stored on your device too.

Some of what you share, such as mood, stress or sleep, can say something about your health. You choose what to share, and we use it only to coach you.

3. How we use it

  • To run your account and sync your data across your devices.
  • To coach you: the coach reads your profile, goals, recent check-ins and coaching notes so its replies fit you.
  • To send the reminders you've switched on.
  • To apply the daily coaching allowance, keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do this because it's needed to provide the app you've signed up for, and, for health-related details you choose to share, with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw by deleting that information or your account. We don't use your data for advertising, sell it, or build profiles for anyone else.

4. The AI coach

The BYT coach is an AI, not a person. Nobody reads your conversations as part of running the app. The coach can misunderstand or be wrong, so treat what it says as a perspective, not a fact.

BYT is a coach, not a therapist or a medical service, and it doesn't diagnose or treat anything. If a conversation suggests you may be at risk, the coach will point you to support. In an emergency, call your local emergency services (999 in the UK) or Samaritans on 116 123.

5. Services we use

A small number of trusted providers process data for us, only to deliver BYT:

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud: sign-in, data storage and the servers that run the coach.
  • Google Gemini API: generates the coach's replies and handles voice. We use Google's paid API, under terms that don't allow Google to use your conversations to train its models. Google may keep requests for a short time to detect abuse.
  • Google Play: distributes the app, under Google's own privacy policy.

Google may process data outside the UK. Where it does, it's protected by Google's data processing terms and the safeguards UK law requires for international transfers.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account, so the coach can remember you. When you delete your account, your profile, goals, check-ins, journal, conversations and coaching notes are permanently deleted straight away. Technical logs expire automatically within 30 days.

7. Your rights and choices

  • See and change your data: most of it is visible and editable in the app. Email us for a full copy.
  • Delete it: in the app, go to Settings → Delete account & data, or follow these steps.
  • Reminders: switch them on or off in Settings → Reminders, or turn off notifications for BYT in your phone's settings.
  • Microphone: only used during voice coaching. You can revoke access in your phone's settings.

Under UK law you also have the right to access, correct, export, restrict or object to how we use your data. To use any of these rights, email us; we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

8. Keeping it safe

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access rules mean only your signed-in account can read your data, and the coach's notes and your allowance can only be written by our servers. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep access to the minimum needed to run BYT.

9. Age

You need to be at least 13 to use BYT. If you believe a younger child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is significant. Questions or requests: eshaantr.mukku@gmail.com.
