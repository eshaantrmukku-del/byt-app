Privacy Policy

A short notice about how BYT handles your information.

Effective 11 July 2026 · Last updated 29 October 2026

We do not sell your personal data. We do not rent or trade it to advertisers, and BYT has no ads or tracking analytics.

What we collect

To run the app we need a few things you provide or create while using BYT:

  • Account details (name, email) so you can sign in
  • What you tell us when you set up your profile, and the goals, check-ins and journal entries you create
  • Your conversations with the coach
  • Short coaching notes the coach keeps so it can remember you between conversations: a summary of past sessions, how you like to be coached, what matters to you, and commitments you've made
  • Voice audio, only while you use voice coaching. It is sent to be transcribed and answered, and we don't keep the recording.
  • How much of your daily coaching allowance you've used

How we use it

We use this information only to provide BYT: keep you signed in, sync your data across devices, power the coach (including remembering your earlier conversations), and run voice coaching when you use it. We don't use it for advertising.

Services we use

BYT runs on Google Firebase (sign-in, data storage and our servers). Coach replies and voice coaching are generated by Google's Gemini models through Google's paid API, which does not use your conversations to train its models. These providers process data only to deliver these features.

Coaching, not therapy

The BYT coach is an AI. It is not a therapist or a medical service. If a conversation suggests you may be at risk, the coach will point you to support services. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

Your choices

You can stop using the app at any time. You can turn off microphone access in your phone settings.

You can delete your account and all its data at any time in the app, under Settings → Delete account & data. You can also visit the account deletion page or email eshaantr.mukku@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my BYT account and data”.
